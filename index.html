Management actions

Stopping a sulphate injection, or removing a sunshade, reverses the cooling within a few years. The ocean takes longer to follow.

Climate states

The game classifies the planet every frame. A state is named from the surface temperature, ice cover, liquid water, pressure and whether the outgoing flux has reached its limit. Some states sit next to each other, so a small push can move a planet from one to the next.

Model and references

The model is a global-mean energy balance with a gray-atmosphere greenhouse (fitted to Earth, Mars and Venus), an IAPWS water-vapour law, a steam-and-ocean energy budget, an outgoing-flux runaway limit, a carbonate-silicate thermostat, hydrogen escape and Gough (1981) solar brightening. It is a teaching model, not a 3-D climate simulation: values are right to within tens of kelvin, and the states are right in order. Details, equations and calibration are in docs/science.md.
